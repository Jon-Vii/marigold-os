MarigoldOS v0.9.1 for Xteink X4 and X3.

### What's new
- The battery saving added in v0.9.0 made most buttons on the X3 stop responding once it was unplugged from USB, so it is turned off on the X3 for now and battery life there is back to v0.8.0. Thanks to Jan Steinke ([@jan-xyz](https://github.com/jan-xyz)), who tracked down the cause in #37.
- Fixes ghosting of the previous screen after changing view or waking. The screen is cleaned with a flash again, so on the X4 a wake takes about 1.6 s instead of half a second.
- Fixes quick taps being missed after a pause.
- Presses made while the reader is going to sleep or waking up are now ignored.

### Updating
| Device | Image | Automatic SD update filename |
| --- | --- | --- |
| X4 | `firmware-x4.bin` | `FWUPDATE.BIN` |
| X3 | `firmware-x3.bin` | `FWUPDX3.BIN` |

Rename the image as shown, put it in the SD-card root, and reboot, or use Settings → Firmware update. For the stock updater, rename it to `update.bin` and follow the [installation instructions](https://github.com/Jon-Vii/marigold-os/blob/v0.9.1/docs/FLASHING.md). USB flashing uses offset `0x10000`.
