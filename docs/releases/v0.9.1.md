MarigoldOS v0.9.1 for Xteink X4 and X3.

### Fixes
- X3: v0.9.0 stopped answering most buttons once the X3 was unplugged from USB. The X3 no longer sleeps between page turns, so on battery it behaves, and drains, as it did in v0.8.0. Thanks to Jan Steinke ([@jan-xyz](https://github.com/jan-xyz)), who found and measured the cause in #37.
- Short taps on Back, Confirm and the previous-page buttons could be missed after a pause. The buttons are checked every 15 ms again, so quick taps register.
- No more ghosting of the previous screen when you change view or wake the reader: both take one flicker again, as before v0.9.0. Page turns and moving through lists stay quick.
- Page turns on battery are about 40 ms quicker, and moving through lists about 100 ms quicker.
- Once the reader starts going to sleep, only the Power button is heard, so a stray press no longer stops the sleep or leaves the screen out of step.
- A press made while the reader is still waking no longer lands in Files, Settings or Wireless.

Waking still returns to the page you were reading. On the X4 a wake within an hour takes about 1.6 s, and a wake from a longer sleep about 2.6 s, in exchange for a clean screen.

### Updating
| Device | Image | Automatic SD update filename |
| --- | --- | --- |
| X4 | `firmware-x4.bin` | `FWUPDATE.BIN` |
| X3 | `firmware-x3.bin` | `FWUPDX3.BIN` |

Rename the image as shown, put it in the SD-card root, and reboot, or use Settings → Firmware update. For the stock updater, rename it to `update.bin` and follow the [installation instructions](https://github.com/Jon-Vii/marigold-os/blob/v0.9.1/docs/FLASHING.md). USB flashing uses offset `0x10000`.
