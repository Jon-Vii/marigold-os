MarigoldOS v0.6.1 for Xteink X4 and X3.

### What's new
- New Alignment setting: Justified (hyphenated, as in v0.6.0) or Left (whole words, even spacing, ragged right).

### Updating
| Device | Image | Automatic SD update filename |
| --- | --- | --- |
| X4 | `firmware-x4.bin` | `FWUPDATE.BIN` |
| X3 | `firmware-x3.bin` | `FWUPDX3.BIN` |

Rename the image as shown, put it in the SD-card root, and reboot, or use Settings → Firmware update. For the stock updater, rename it to `update.bin` and follow the [installation instructions](https://github.com/Jon-Vii/marigold-os/blob/v0.6.1/docs/FLASHING.md). USB flashing uses offset `0x10000`.

On the X4, books keep their cache. Changing the alignment rebuilds each book the first time you open it, and on the X3 each book rebuilds once after updating. Saved positions are kept.
