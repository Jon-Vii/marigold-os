MarigoldOS v0.7.0 for Xteink X4 and X3.

### What's new
- Images in books: PNG and JPEG, dithered for the panel. Illustrations sit between paragraphs at their own size, and small images such as symbols and decorated first letters sit inside the line of text.
- Images are prepared in the background, starting with the pages just ahead of you, so there's no waiting or pop-in in normal reading.
- Faster SD card access: line-spacing changes and Wi-Fi uploads are quicker.
- Fixes plain words at the start of a line being drawn in italic or bold when the line contains italic or bold later on.
- Fixes a button press right after waking or booting that could stop books from showing until the next restart.
- Thanks to Jan Steinke ([@jan-xyz](https://github.com/jan-xyz)), whose prototype in #17 started the image work.

### Updating
| Device | Image | Automatic SD update filename |
| --- | --- | --- |
| X4 | `firmware-x4.bin` | `FWUPDATE.BIN` |
| X3 | `firmware-x3.bin` | `FWUPDX3.BIN` |

Rename the image as shown, put it in the SD-card root, and reboot, or use Settings → Firmware update. For the stock updater, rename it to `update.bin` and follow the [installation instructions](https://github.com/Jon-Vii/marigold-os/blob/v0.7.0/docs/FLASHING.md). USB flashing uses offset `0x10000`.

Updating from v0.6.x, books without images keep their cache, and books with images rebuild once the first time you open them. Books cached by older versions rebuild once. Saved positions are kept.
