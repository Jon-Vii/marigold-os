MarigoldOS v0.6.0 keeps your place more reliably, wakes faster, and fixes lost text in some EPUBs on Xteink X4 and X3.

### Reading
- Keep the reading place when a type-size, weight, face, spacing, or orientation change lays the book out again, and when opening a book last read under other settings. Previously the page number stayed the same and the text moved.
- Open books straight onto their saved page in one refresh; reopening a book from the Library no longer shows its first page first.
- Wake onto the reading screen with the one-flicker clean refresh (about 2 s faster on X4). "Fast only" is the new default refresh setting; "full on wake" keeps the multi-flash refresh on every wake. Settings saved with the old default move to the new one.
- Justify reading lines on the device.
- Lighter italic glyph cut so italics match the weight of roman text.
- With no books on the card, Continue opens the Library, which explains how to add books, and Home no longer titles a placeholder book.

### Books and the card
- Stop losing text: book packages larger than 16 KB keep all their chapters, comments and CDATA no longer swallow text, spaces around styled words are kept, list paragraphs, horizontal rules, self-closing tags and HTML named entities are handled, and a damaged chapter is skipped instead of ending the book.
- Books added to the card with a card reader appear in the Library without a manual rescan; empty (0-byte) EPUBs left by an interrupted upload are skipped.
- Deleting a book frees all of its space on the card, and leftover caches of removed books are swept.
- Far chapter jumps keep their page; a book reopened at a saved page reports the right chapter.

### Reliability
- Reading positions are written within 15 seconds of the last page turn and before switching books; going to sleep always completes, even after a failed card write or panel refresh.
- A Power press still held while the reader wakes no longer sends it back to sleep.
- After an unexpected error the reader restarts instead of halting.
- The firmware updater rejects images built for another chip; the Wi-Fi upload page and captive network answers are hardened.

### Validation and limits
Validated on X4: wake to the restored page in 3.0 s from boot (5.0 s with "full on wake"); reopening a prepared book in one refresh; a spacing change (1,367 → 1,638 pages) and a type-size rebuild (1,367 → 1,910 pages) each kept the reading place and mapped back exactly; 50 consecutive forward page turns at 497 ms median; peak stack use 27.5 KB of 36.6 KB. X3 release builds and host tests passed; X3 hardware was not available. The kept place is accurate to about a page; if the reader sleeps partway through a layout rebuild, it falls back to the saved page number.

### Updating
Download the image matching your device:

| Device | Image | Automatic Marigold SD update filename |
| --- | --- | --- |
| X4 | `firmware-x4.bin` | `FWUPDATE.BIN` |
| X3 | `firmware-x3.bin` | `FWUPDX3.BIN` |

For Marigold's automatic SD update, rename the selected image as shown, place it in the SD-card root, and reboot normally. Alternatively, use Settings → Firmware update with the canonical `.bin` filename.

For the stock/OEM SD updater, rename the correct device image to `update.bin` and follow the [installation instructions](https://github.com/Jon-Vii/marigold-os/blob/v0.6.0/docs/FLASHING.md). The website supplies the appropriate download filename automatically.

These are app-only images. USB flashing uses offset `0x10000` and preserves the bootloader and partition table. Book caches rebuild once on first open after updating (the layout version changed); saved reading positions are retained.
