MarigoldOS v0.6.0 for Xteink X4 and X3.

### What's new
- Keeps your place when you change type settings or orientation.
- Opens new books at the start of the text, past the cover and front matter.
- Wakes faster, with a single quick refresh instead of the full flashing one. "Fast only" is the new default refresh setting.
- Justified text and lighter italics.
- Long books load in full (up to 1,024 chapter files, was 192).
- Fixes missing text in some EPUBs.
- Books copied with a card reader show up without a rescan.
- Reading positions save more reliably.
- Plus smaller fixes throughout.

### Updating
| Device | Image | Automatic SD update filename |
| --- | --- | --- |
| X4 | `firmware-x4.bin` | `FWUPDATE.BIN` |
| X3 | `firmware-x3.bin` | `FWUPDX3.BIN` |

Rename the image as shown, put it in the SD-card root, and reboot, or use Settings → Firmware update. For the stock updater, rename it to `update.bin` and follow the [installation instructions](https://github.com/Jon-Vii/marigold-os/blob/v0.6.0/docs/FLASHING.md). USB flashing uses offset `0x10000`.

Each book rebuilds its cache the first time you open it, which can take a few minutes for a long book. Saved positions are kept.
