MarigoldOS v0.6.0 keeps your place more reliably, wakes faster, and reads more of your books correctly on Xteink X4 and X3.

### Reading
- Keep your place in the text when changing type size, weight, face, line spacing, or orientation, and when opening a book last read with other settings.
- Open a new book where its text begins, after the cover and front matter. Front matter is now kept: page back or use Contents to read a foreword or dedication.
- Wake about 2 s faster on X4. "Fast only" is the new default refresh setting; choose "full on wake" to keep the full refresh on every wake.
- Justify text on the device, and draw italics at the same weight as regular text.

### Books and the card
- Fix missing text in some EPUBs: chapters dropped from books with large package files, text swallowed by some markup, lost spaces around italic or bold words, and lists, scene breaks, and special characters that did not show.
- Load long books in full: up to 1,024 chapter files instead of 192, so collections such as Rationality (381 essays) are no longer cut short.
- Show books copied with a card reader without a rescan.
- Free all of a deleted book's space on the card.

### Reliability
- Save your reading position more reliably: within 15 seconds of a page turn, before switching books, and when going to sleep, even after a card or display error.
- Restart after an unexpected error instead of freezing.
- Smaller fixes to opening, resuming, and waking, safer firmware updates and Wi-Fi uploads, and a clearer start with an empty card.

### Validation and limits
Tested on X4: wake in 3.0 s from boot (5.0 s with "full on wake"); type-size and spacing changes kept the reading place and returned to the same page when reverted; 50 forward page turns at a 497 ms median; Rationality (2,977 pages) and a 234-essay collection built in full. X3 builds and automated tests passed, but X3 hardware was not available. After a layout change, your place is kept to within about a page.

### Updating
Download the image matching your device:

| Device | Image | Automatic Marigold SD update filename |
| --- | --- | --- |
| X4 | `firmware-x4.bin` | `FWUPDATE.BIN` |
| X3 | `firmware-x3.bin` | `FWUPDX3.BIN` |

For Marigold's automatic SD update, rename the selected image as shown, place it in the SD-card root, and reboot normally. Alternatively, use Settings → Firmware update with the canonical `.bin` filename.

For the stock/OEM SD updater, rename the correct device image to `update.bin` and follow the [installation instructions](https://github.com/Jon-Vii/marigold-os/blob/v0.6.0/docs/FLASHING.md). The website supplies the appropriate download filename automatically.

These are app-only images. USB flashing uses offset `0x10000` and preserves the bootloader and partition table. Each book rebuilds its cache the first time you open it after updating, which can take several minutes for a long book; saved reading positions are kept.
