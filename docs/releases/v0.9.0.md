MarigoldOS v0.9.0 for Xteink X4 and X3.

### What's new
- Longer battery life while reading: between page turns the reader now sleeps, so it drains roughly 3–7× slower.
- Waking within an hour of putting the reader to sleep returns to your page in about half a second, with no reboot.
- After a longer sleep the reader boots straight to the page you were reading. On the X4 that takes one quick refresh instead of a flash.
- Going between a book and Home is quicker on the X4: the panel is no longer cleaned every time you change view.

### Updating
| Device | Image | Automatic SD update filename |
| --- | --- | --- |
| X4 | `firmware-x4.bin` | `FWUPDATE.BIN` |
| X3 | `firmware-x3.bin` | `FWUPDX3.BIN` |

Rename the image as shown, put it in the SD-card root, and reboot, or use Settings → Firmware update. For the stock updater, rename it to `update.bin` and follow the [installation instructions](https://github.com/Jon-Vii/marigold-os/blob/v0.9.0/docs/FLASHING.md). USB flashing uses offset `0x10000`.
